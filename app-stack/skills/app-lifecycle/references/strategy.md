# The product strategy (optional)

**What it is for:** where the product is going, as the owner has confirmed it, with the date of that confirmation.

**Read it** when a decision turns on direction rather than on what the product does today.

**Rules**

- **Optional. Leave it a stub until the owner confirms a strategy.**
- **Never fill it with a draft the owner has not confirmed.** The brief describes the product as it is meant to work now; a guess written here would be read by every later session as a commitment.
- **Read it only once the owner has confirmed one** — an unconfirmed strategy is not an input to a decision.
